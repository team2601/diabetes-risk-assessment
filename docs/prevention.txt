Preventing Type 2 Diabetes
https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/preventing-type-2-diabetes
Overview
You can prevent or delay type 2 diabetes by making sustainable lifestyle changes, especially if you have prediabetes or are overweight. Research from the Diabetes Prevention Program (DPP) showed that modest weight loss and regular physical activity dramatically lower type 2 diabetes risk.

Key steps to prevent or delay type 2 diabetes
1. Lose weight and keep it off
- Losing 5 to 7 percent of your starting body weight can prevent or delay type 2 diabetes. For example, if you weigh 200 pounds, losing 10 to 14 pounds makes a significant difference.
- Focus on gradual, realistic changes rather than extreme fad diets.

2. Move more every week
- Aim for at least 30 minutes of physical activity 5 days a week (150 minutes per week total).
- If you have not been active, start slowly—even 10 minutes at a time—and build up gradually. Brisk walking, cycling, swimming, and dancing all count.
- Reduce long periods of sitting throughout the day.

3. Follow a healthy eating plan
- Eat smaller portions to lower total calories and support weight management.
- Choose foods lower in saturated fat, trans fat, and added sugars.
- Fill your plate with vegetables, fruits, whole grains, beans, and lean proteins.
- Replace sugar-sweetened drinks such as soda and sweet tea with water.

4. Manage blood pressure and overall health
- Keep blood pressure and cholesterol in healthy ranges through nutrition, exercise, and medical follow-up.
- Avoid smoking and get adequate sleep.

5. Ask your health care provider about additional support
- Join a lifestyle change program with trained coaches and peer support.
- For some people at very high risk of developing type 2 diabetes (such as those with obesity or a history of gestational diabetes), a doctor may also discuss the prescription medication metformin alongside lifestyle changes.
- If you had gestational diabetes, get tested for type 2 diabetes 6 to 12 weeks after your baby is born, and then at least every 3 years for life.
